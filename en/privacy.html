Privacy Policy

Hyeonu Kim (the “developer”) explains below how information is handled in the game “Popo's Big Adventure / ポポ大冒険” (formerly “Popo's Little Room”) and on this support site.

1. Information the game collects or transmits

When you first launch the game, you sign in with Apple (Sign in with Apple) or Google to create your game account. There are no ads and no analytics tools added by the developer. Your game progress (companions, levels, gear, stages) is stored on your device, and your Star Candy is stored with your account (sections 8 and 10). Your account is created only when you agree to the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy at your first sign-in. When you choose a name in the Friends tab and use the Friends features, Battle or Ranking, that information is stored on the server (sections 8 to 12). The first sign-in needs an internet connection; after that you can play without one (changes to your Star Candy are sent to your account once you are connected).

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress (companions, levels, gear, stages), settings, and the photos you take in the game together with each photo's scene text (section 8) are stored in the app's storage on your device. Save data from an earlier version is left as it is if it is on the device. They are used to continue your game and display it on screen; the developer has no access to this data.

Photos in the game are drawn from the game screen and do not use your device's camera. The microphone is requested the first time you use voice in a room. The first time you tap “Save” in the album, the app asks for permission to add photos to the Photos app (add only); it never reads the photos in your library. Notification permission is requested as described under “Notifications” in section 8. The game does not request access to your contacts or location.

The sign-in details of your account are stored in the Keychain on iOS and in the app's private storage on other devices.

3. Retention, deletion and backups

On-device data is kept for as long as the app's data remains. You can delete the app and its data for that device in iOS Settings. “Offload App” may keep the data and is not the same as deleting it.

Data that exists only on your device (such as game progress) is not collected by the developer, so the developer cannot delete or restore it remotely. Operating-system backups and diagnostics are handled according to your device and Apple's settings and policies. The developer provides no cloud save for game progress, and recovery after reinstalling is not guaranteed. Your account and Star Candy carry over after reinstalling or on another device when you sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

4. Email enquiries

If you email us, we use your email address, your message and anything you attach to reply and resolve the problem. Contacting us is optional and is not required to play. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal information.

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to respond and resolve the issue, then deleted when no longer necessary, unless a legal retention duty applies. To request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry records, contact the email address below.

5. Support site and external services

This site has no contact form, no advertising and no visitor analytics scripts installed by the developer. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser information and access logs to deliver pages and operate securely. Tapping an email link opens your mail app, and that email service's policies apply.

This site is served through GitHub Pages. For the provider's handling of information, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Children's information

Playing requires signing in with Apple or Google. The only user information received then is the Apple user ID or Google user ID; no name, e-mail address or age is collected. For children under 13 using the Friends features, see section 12. Please do not send unnecessary personal information about children in email enquiries. A parent or guardian may request deletion of information using the contact below.

7. Changes and contact

If features or data handling change, this page and its revision date will be updated. Information handled by online features is described in sections 8–12.

8. Information handled by online features

When you first launch the game, sign in with Apple or Google and agree to the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy, an account is created. When you sign up for the first time, until you agree, the sign-in proof from Apple or Google is only checked and not stored, and no account is created (only the request counts that include the IP address, kept to limit requests, remain, and they are deleted after two days). When you sign in to or link an account that already exists (including one made in an earlier version), the information below is stored with that account. If an account was already made with the same Apple ID or Google account, you are signed in to that account, so you keep the same account after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. An account made with Apple and an account made with Google are separate accounts and are never merged. The only user information received from Apple or Google is the Apple user ID for this app or the Google user ID; your name, e-mail address and profile photo are not requested (the tokens used to check sign-ins and to disconnect are described below). If an account made in an earlier version is on the device, the sign-in you choose is linked to that account and you keep using it. The following information is stored on the server. A sign-in record for each device you sign in on, and, for an account that uses Sign in with Apple, a token Apple provides so that Sign in with Apple can be disconnected when you delete your account, are also kept.

  • Account: a random account number and friend code, a hash of the sign-in token, the time you were last online (used to show your friends whether you are online now), the Apple user ID or Google user ID, and the version of the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy you agreed to and when. A “Move to another device” code from an earlier version can be used for 10 minutes. Its hash is deleted when the code is used or the account is deleted, and replaced when a new code is issued.
  • Friends: your friend list, friend requests and blocked players.
  • Letters: the letters you and your friends write to each other (up to 500 characters), the paper chosen, and when each was sent and arrives. Photos and drawings cannot be added, and letters containing links, phone numbers or e-mail addresses are refused by the server.
  • Playing together: play invitations (who invited whom and when). Characters' positions and moves in a room pass between the two of you in real time and are not stored.
  • Lead character and progress: When you use the Friends tab, your lead character (the one you chose under “Me” in the Friends tab, or the character at the front of your party if you have not chosen) and your progress (the highest difficulty you have opened and the number of stages cleared in it) are stored on the server. Progress is used to choose friend recommendations; the values used for Battle and Ranking are listed under “Battle and Ranking” below.
  • Profile photo: When you set a photo taken in the game as your profile photo, no image is uploaded. Only the photo's scene text (which characters stand where and in what pose, the place, filter, frame, stickers and the position of the cut-out circle, up to 600 characters) is stored on the server. The server checks that it refers only to the game's own art, and it can be changed up to 30 times a day. Other players' games redraw it from the game's art in the friend list, received requests, recommendation cards, letters, rooms, invitations, Battle and Ranking. “Remove photo” deletes it.
  • Friend recommendations: Your name and lead character (or profile photo, if you have set one) may be shown as a recommendation to friends of your friends and to players whose progress is close to yours. A recommendation shows only the name and picture. This stays the same after a friend request is sent, until the other player accepts it.
  • Room chat: Short messages (40 characters per line) written while you are in a room with a friend pass through the server to both of you. Messages containing links, phone numbers or e-mail addresses are not delivered, and banned words such as insults are masked with *. The room keeps the last 20 lines so that reports can be reviewed, and deletes them two minutes after both players have left.
  • Banned words in letters and names: Banned words in letters are stored masked with *. A name containing a banned word cannot be used.
  • Notifications: When a friend sends you a letter or invites you to their room, we let you know with an iPhone notification. When you first enter the game after signing in, your iPhone asks whether to allow notifications. If you allow them, we store on the server, with your account, your device's notification token (a value Apple issues for this app on this device), the language of the notifications, and whether “Notifications” is on in the game's settings. A notification shows only your friend's name and “Sent you a letter” or “Invited you to their room”; it never contains what a letter says. So that tapping it opens that letter or room, the notification also carries your friend's account number and the number of the letter or room. Notifications are delivered through Apple's notification service (APNs). You do not get notifications from people you have blocked. If you do not allow notifications, or turn them off in the game's settings, the game and the Friends features work as before.
  • Battle and Ranking: your companions' levels and worn gear, training levels, your battle team, this week's adventure score, battle records (opponent, result, rating change) and your rating. Sent only after you choose a name in the Friends tab. Other players can see your name, lead character or profile photo, rating, battle team and its power, and your ranking values (stage reached, this week's adventure score, power, rating). Battle records are deleted after 30 days; everything else is deleted with your account.

Accounts that used the pet features of the earlier version (“Popo's Little Room”) also have the following information stored.

  • Pet and profile: your pet's species, look, growth stage and name; the introduction you write (60 characters); one picture you took in the game and chose for your profile; the number of adoption slots you bought and when the baby look ends. Banned words in the introduction are stored masked with *.
  • Marriage and children: proposals and online marriage records, and the species, look, name and growth stage of children born to a married pair. While a child is being sent to the other side, its game data is kept on the server until it is received.
  • Strangers (passers-by) see only your pet; its name, your introduction and your photo are shown to friends only.

Handled without consent (information needed to provide the account and payments): the Apple user ID or Google user ID, the account number and friend code (the friend code is set by the server when the account is created; it is not sent to your device until you agree to the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy at your first sign-in, and it is used only when you give it to someone yourself), the version of the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy you agreed to and when, a sign-in record for each device, the Apple token used to disconnect Sign in with Apple when the account is deleted (accounts that use Sign in with Apple), the Star Candy balance and its history (rewards received, amounts used, the days of Daily Star Candy), purchase and refund records (transaction number, product, time), and request counts that include the IP address, kept to limit requests (deleted after two days). These are used to sign you in, deliver what you bought, make sure the same purchase is not used twice, reflect refunds and delete the account.

Handled when you use the Friends features, Battle or Ranking (needed to provide them, handled without consent): your name, your lead character and progress, your profile photo's scene text, friends, friend requests and blocks, letters, room chat, report records, invitations to play together, the notification token and setting, and the Battle and Ranking information. The notification token, language and setting are sent once you allow notifications (even before you choose a name); the rest is sent only when you use those features, starting with choosing a name in the Friends tab. Voice is only relayed and never stored, and the device asks separately for the microphone the first time you use voice.

9. Voice chat

  • The first time you use voice in a room, the app asks for microphone access, and your voice is sent to your friend's device in real time (WebRTC, encrypted).
  • Your voice always goes through Cloudflare's relay servers (TURN), so your friend never sees your IP address. The relay only passes the encrypted audio along and does not store it. Relay credentials are issued only for the time you are allowed to talk.
  • Your voice is never recorded or stored. The server keeps only how many minutes of voice you used each day (to count the free time and to watch running costs; kept for eight days) and short per-room permission records (so that an account uses voice in only one room at a time).

10. Purchases, subscription and gifts

  • Star Candy and Daily Star Candy, the Talk Pass (monthly subscription), and the earlier version's baby look, photo space, adoption slots, one-time rename and 30-day gift pass are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive card numbers or other payment details.
  • The server confirms each purchase with the store and stores the store's transaction number, the product, the purchase time and the subscription's expiry time with your account. Renewals, cancellations and refunds are applied from notifications Apple sends to the server (App Store Server Notifications) and from store lookups.
  • So that the Talk Pass applies to the game accounts on every device that uses the same Apple Account, the server stores the link between the subscription's transactions and the game accounts that use it (up to 10).
  • A Talk Pass gift records the sender, the recipient, the time and the number of days. The recipient sees the name of the sender's pet, and the record is used to adjust the recipient's days if the purchase is refunded.
  • Star Candy: Your account stores your Star Candy balance (bought and received kept apart) and its history (the names and amounts of rewards received, amounts used, purchases, refunds, and the days of Daily Star Candy). This information is used only to deliver Star Candy, to carry it over with the same Apple ID or Google account when you reinstall the app or move to a new device, to make sure the same reward or purchase is not used twice, and to reflect refunds. Star Candy that an earlier version stored on your device is moved to your account once, when you first sign in.
  • Star Candy purchases: Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. So that purchased Star Candy is delivered and is never given twice or lost, a purchase is always recorded with an account. If an unfinished purchase arrives before you sign in, it is recorded with your account after you sign in. The server checks that a purchase is genuine and then stores the transaction number, product and purchase time with the account. When a refund happens, the transaction number, product, refund type and time from Apple's notification are recorded, and the Star Candy that purchase gave is taken back from the account. If you do not use the Friends tab, no Friends information is sent (except the notification token, language and setting once you allow notifications).

11. Reports, moderation and abuse prevention

  • A report stores its reason and, when you report a letter, that letter. Only reports from friends or from people who actually exchanged letters or played with the account are counted.
  • When reports pass a threshold, that account's introduction is hidden for a while and marked “under review”. An operator reviews the reports together with that account's recent letters, introduction and profile photo and decides whether to keep or lift restrictions; sending letters is restricted only after an operator confirms.
  • A reported player is told that their account is under review and can ask for another review (the request text is stored). We never tell them who reported them.
  • To prevent abuse and runaway costs, the IP address of each request is used briefly for rate limiting, and each account's daily request count is kept for eight days. To limit how often requests can be made, the server keeps counters that include the IP address and deletes them after two days. Operational logs for troubleshooting may remain at Cloudflare for a few days.
  • When you report someone from inside a room, the reported player's most recent messages in that room (up to 20 lines and 1,000 characters) are stored with the report. You can report from the friend list, a recommendation card, a received request, a letter, a room or the Battle screens, and you can block the player at the same time. A blocked player is left out of your battle opponents, battle records and notifications.

12. Retention, deletion and service providers

  • Online information is kept while the account exists. You can delete the account at any time with “Delete account” at the bottom of “Me” in the Friends tab (before you choose a name, it is at the bottom of the Friends tab itself). Deleting it immediately deletes the account, the sign-in records on the server, your name, lead character and progress, profile photo scene text, profile and photo, friends, letters, reports, invitations, children records, subscription link, received gifts, usage records, notification tokens, battle records and ranking information, your Star Candy balance and its history, and any Daily Star Candy not yet received, and closes any room you are in. For an account that uses Sign in with Apple, it also asks Apple to revoke the token, which disconnects Sign in with Apple. If Apple does not answer at once, the token is kept without any link to the account and the request is retried a limited number of times, then the token is deleted. Purchase records for photo space and adoption slots and the subscription link are deleted, so the purchases can be restored on a new account. Your game progress and the album on your device stay on the device. If you sign in again with the same Apple ID or Google account after deleting, you are asked to agree to the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy again, and a new, empty account is created when you agree. A Talk Pass subscription keeps billing after the account is deleted, so please cancel it under App Store subscription management.
  • Exceptions: other payment confirmation records (transaction number and product) remain without any link to you, so the same payment cannot be used twice. Gifts you sent remain without your details, for the recipient's days. If you are married, the marriage record remains marked as ended so that your partner's device can reflect it. So that a sign-in proof cannot be used again, a hash of each used proof is kept for one day without any link to an account, then deleted. Counts of letters sent (kept to limit requests, with the sending and receiving account numbers) may remain after an account is deleted and are deleted after two days.
  • Your device's notification token moves to the other account if you sign in to another account on the same device. We also delete it when Apple tells us it can no longer be used (for example, after the app was deleted). Battle records are deleted after 30 days.
  • The server and database are operated by Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, D1, Durable Objects and Realtime TURN for voice relay); data may be processed in Cloudflare data centers, including in the United States. To confirm payments, transaction details are checked with Apple or Google. To check sign-ins and to disconnect Sign in with Apple, the server communicates with Apple's and Google's sign-in services, and notifications are delivered through Apple's notification service (APNs).
  • Children under 13 should use the Friends features (name, letters, room chat, voice) with a parent's consent. A parent can ask for deletion using the contact in section 7. The account information (section 8) and the Star Candy history are used only for the game's internal operations (signing in, delivering Star Candy, carrying it over after a reinstall or a move to a new device, and handling duplicates and refunds) and for the features described in this policy. The developer does not use them for advertising, does not share them with other services or companies for advertising or marketing (service providers that run the service are as described above), does not link them to a real name or contact details, and does not use them to contact a specific person or to build a profile of a person. The game has no ads or analytics tools.
