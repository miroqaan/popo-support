Privacy Policy

Hyeonu Kim (the “developer”) explains below how information is handled in the game “Popo's Little Room / 포포의 작은 방 / ポポの小さなお部屋” (its offline configuration and online features) and on this support site.

1. Information the game collects or transmits

The game has no account registration or login. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics tools installed by the developer, and it does not send gameplay information to a developer server. The above describes the offline configuration, with online features turned off. When you turn on online features, sections 8–12 below also apply.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress, the name you give your friend, sound settings, save times, play records, garden and cooking state, and in-game memory photos are stored in the app's storage on your device. They are used to continue your game and display it on screen; the developer has no access to this data.

Memory photos (the pet camera) save the game screen and do not use your device's camera. The microphone is requested only when you press Talk while playing together. The game does not request access to your contacts, location or photo library.

With online features on, the sign-in details of your online account are stored in the Keychain on iOS and in the app's private storage on other devices.

3. Retention, deletion and backups

On-device data is kept for as long as the app's data remains. You can delete the app and its data for that device in iOS Settings. “Offload App” may keep the data and is not the same as deleting it.

Because the developer does not collect device data, the developer cannot delete or restore it remotely. Operating-system backups and diagnostics are handled according to your device and Apple's settings and policies. The developer provides no cloud save or device transfer for game progress, and recovery after reinstalling is not guaranteed. Your online account can be moved with “Move to another device” (section 8).

4. Email enquiries

If you email us, we use your email address, your message and anything you attach to reply and resolve the problem. Contacting us is optional and is not required to play. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal information.

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to respond and resolve the issue, then deleted when no longer necessary, unless a legal retention duty applies. To request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry records, contact the email address below.

5. Support site and external services

This site has no contact form, no advertising and no visitor analytics scripts installed by the developer. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser information and access logs to deliver pages and operate securely. Tapping an email link opens your mail app, and that email service's policies apply.

This site is served through GitHub Pages. For the provider's handling of information, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Children's information

The game does not require anyone, of any age, to submit personal information in order to play. Please do not send unnecessary personal information about children in email enquiries. A parent or guardian may request deletion of enquiry information using the contact below.

7. Changes and contact

8. Information handled by online features

Opening online friends creates an anonymous account for the device. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number. The server stores:

  • Account: a random account number and friend code, a hash of the sign-in token, the time you were last online and, if you link Sign in with Apple, the user identifier Apple provides. When you use “Move to another device”, a hash of the move code is kept for 10 minutes and deleted once it is used.
  • Pet and profile: your pet's species, look, growth stage and name; the introduction you write (60 characters); one picture you took in the game and chose for your profile; the number of adoption slots you bought and when the baby look ends.
  • Friends: your friend list, friend requests, blocked players, proposals and online marriage records, and the species, look, name and growth stage of children born to a married pair. While a child is being sent to the other side, its game data is kept on the server until it is received.
  • Letters: the letters you and your friends write to each other, the paper chosen, and when each was sent and arrives. A letter reaches the friend's mailbox a little while after it is sent. Photos and drawings cannot be added, and letters containing links, phone numbers or e-mail addresses are refused by the server.
  • Playing together: play invitations (who invited whom and when). Your pet's position, moves and emotes while playing pass between the two of you in real time and are not stored.

Strangers (passers-by) see only your pet; its name, your introduction and your photo are shown to friends only.

9. Voice chat

  • When you press Talk while playing together, the app asks for microphone access and your voice is sent to your friend's device in real time (WebRTC, encrypted).
  • Your voice always goes through Cloudflare's relay servers (TURN), so your friend never sees your IP address. The relay only passes the encrypted audio along and does not store it. Relay credentials are issued only for the time you are allowed to talk.
  • Your voice is never recorded or stored. The server keeps only how many minutes of voice you used each day (to count the free time and the pass limit; kept for eight days) and short per-room permission records.

10. Purchases, subscription and gifts

  • The baby look, photo space, adoption slots, a one-time rename, the Talk Pass (monthly subscription) and the 30-day gift pass are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive card numbers or other payment details.
  • The server confirms each purchase with the store and stores the store's transaction number, the product, the purchase time and the subscription's expiry time with your account. Renewals, cancellations and refunds are applied from notifications Apple sends to the server (App Store Server Notifications) and from store lookups.
  • A Talk Pass gift records the sender, the recipient, the time and the number of days. The recipient sees the name of the sender's pet, and the record is used to adjust the recipient's days if the purchase is refunded.

11. Reports, moderation and abuse prevention

  • A report stores its reason and, when you report a letter, that letter. Only reports from friends or from people who actually exchanged letters or played with the account are counted.
  • When reports pass a threshold, that account's introduction is hidden for a while and marked “under review”. An operator reviews the reports together with that account's recent letters, introduction and profile photo and decides whether to keep or lift restrictions; sending letters is restricted only after an operator confirms.
  • A reported player is told that their account is under review and can ask for another review (the request text is stored). We never tell them who reported them.
  • To prevent abuse and runaway costs, the IP address of each request is used briefly for rate limiting, and each account's daily request count is kept for eight days. We do not store IP addresses ourselves, but operational logs for diagnosing errors may remain with Cloudflare for a few days.

12. Retention, deletion and service providers

  • Online information is kept while your account exists. “Profile → Delete online account” in the app immediately deletes your account, profile, photo, friends, letters, reports, invitations, children records, subscription link, received gifts and usage records. Purchase records for photo space and adoption slots and the subscription link are deleted, so you can restore those purchases on a new account.
  • Exceptions: other payment confirmation records (transaction number and product) remain without any link to you, so the same payment cannot be used twice. Gifts you sent remain without your details, for the recipient's days. If you are married, the marriage record remains marked as ended so that your partner's device can reflect it.
  • The server and database are operated by Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, D1, Durable Objects and Realtime TURN for voice relay); data may be processed in Cloudflare data centers, including in the United States. To confirm payments, transaction details are checked with Apple or Google.
  • Children under 13 should use online features with a parent's or guardian's consent. A parent or guardian can ask for deletion at the contact in section 7.
