Support

King Rumble has put out the town's Star Lantern. In this idle game, Popo and friends set off on their own adventure to light it again.

Account and sign-in

When you first start, you sign in with Apple or Google to create your account. Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account and you keep the same account and Star Candy after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. An account made with Apple and an account made with Google are separate accounts.

Game progress is saved on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Restoring purchases

If Star Candy you paid for has not arrived, tap “Restore Purchases” at the bottom of “Star Candy” in the Shop tab.

For the Talk Pass, open “Talk Pass” under “Me” in the Friends tab and tap “Restore purchases”. You can cancel the subscription under Subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Deleting your account

Use “Delete account” at the bottom of “Me” in the Friends tab. Before you choose a name, it is at the bottom of the Friends tab itself. A deleted account cannot be restored. A Talk Pass subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account, so please cancel it in the App Store.

Notifications

To turn off notifications for friends' letters and room invites, tap the gear at the top right and switch off “Notifications” in “Settings”. You can also turn them off under Notifications in the iPhone's Settings.

Contact

If something goes wrong, tell us your device model, iOS version, game version and what you were doing when the problem happened. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment information.
