Support

A gentle virtual-pet game about caring for a little friend, gardening, cooking and going on picnics. This page covers Popo's Little Room, playable in English, Korean and Japanese.

Getting started

  1. Listen to the eggs in the grass, choose one to bring home, then gently tap it five times to meet your friend.
  2. Give your friend a name, then begin caring for them with food, play and gentle strokes.
  3. Discover new activities and memories in the garden, kitchen, outings and photo album.

Saving and recovery

Progress and settings are saved automatically on your device. No login is required, and the developer does not provide cloud saves or device-to-device transfer.

If something goes wrong, close the app and open it again. Deleting the app may erase your save data, so please contact us before reinstalling. Restoring from an operating-system backup depends on your device settings and is not guaranteed.

Sound and care

You can adjust sound from Settings at the top right. Food and treatment are free. While you play, your friend may become sick or eventually say goodbye depending on their care, but they never pass away while the app is closed.

Contact us

If you run into a problem, please tell us your device model, iOS version, game version and what you were doing when it happened. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment information.
