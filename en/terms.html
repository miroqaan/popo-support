These terms apply to everyone who uses Popo's Big Adventure (the "game"). Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement and these terms both apply to the game.

2. Account

When you first start the game, you sign in with Apple or Google, and your account is created when you agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy. There is one game account per Apple ID or Google account, and signing in with the same Apple ID or Google account gives you the same account on other devices. An account made with Apple and an account made with Google are separate accounts and are never merged. Your account and the Star Candy, Daily Star Candy and Talk Pass on it cannot be given or sold to anyone else.

3. Payments

Star Candy, Daily Star Candy and the Talk Pass are paid for through the App Store. Star Candy can be used only in the game and cannot be exchanged for money. Refunds follow Apple's process, and Star Candy received from a refunded purchase is taken back from the account. The Talk Pass is an auto-renewable subscription; cancel it in the App Store's subscription settings.

4. Friends features, Battle and Ranking

In the Friends features your name and your lead character or profile photo are shown to others, and friends exchange letters, room chat and voice. In Battle and Ranking your name, your lead character or profile photo, your battle team and its power, and your ranking values are shown to others.

  • Reports are reviewed by the operator. Words that break these terms are hidden, and users who break them have their Friends features or account restricted.

5. Not allowed

Cheating on payments, exploiting bugs, using programs that play automatically, pretending to be someone else, sending excessive requests to the server, and breaking the law are not allowed. If you do, Friends features or the account may be restricted.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time with "Delete account" at the bottom of "Me" in the Friends tab (before you choose a name, it is at the bottom of the Friends tab itself). Deleting it erases your name, friends, letters, battle records, Star Candy and any Daily Star Candy not yet received, and cannot be undone. A Talk Pass subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account, so please cancel it in the App Store.
